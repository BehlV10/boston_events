🎭 RUNNING ALL WEEKEND

  • Late Nite Catechism: Til Death Do Us Part @ Charles Playhouse Theater 2 · Fri–Sun · $55 · Theater District
    Long-running audience-participation comedy: Sister's catechism class, this time on marriage.
  • THE BEAUTY QUEEN OF LEENANE @ Calderwood Pavilion · Fri–Sun · South End
    Martin McDonagh's pitch-black comedy-thriller about a mother and daughter in rural Connemara.
  • You're A Good Man, Charlie Brown @ BCA Plaza Theatre · Fri–Sun · South End
    The Peanuts musical, up close at the BCA Plaza Theatre in the South End.
  • Wicked (Touring) @ Citizens Opera House · Fri–Sun · Downtown Crossing
    The Broadway blockbuster's national tour at the Citizens Bank Opera House.
  • Hadestown (Touring) @ Emerson Colonial Theatre · Fri–Sat · Theater District
    Anaïs Mitchell's haunting, Tony-winning underworld folk opera, at the Emerson Colonial through Nov 14.
  • Boston Art Book Fair Free Fun @ Cyclorama · Sat–Sun · Free · South End
    Free weekend fair of zines, artist books and small-press prints filling the domed Cyclorama in the South End.
  • Peter Pan Goes Wrong @ Lyric Stage Company · Sat–Sun · Back Bay
    Slapstick farce from the creators of 'The Play That Goes Wrong', at the Lyric Stage.
