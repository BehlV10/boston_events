🎭 RUNNING ALL WEEKEND

  • The Dambuilders - TWO SHOWS with Special Guests @ The Burren · Fri–Sat · $29 · Davis Sq
    90s Boston indie-rock favorites reunite for two nights at the Burren.
  • Letters To Cleo (18+) @ Paradise Rock Club · Fri–Sat · Allston
    Boston's 90s alt-rock heroes ('Here & Now') play two hometown nights.
  • The Sound of Music @ Boch Center Wang Theatre · Fri–Sun · Theater District
    The Rodgers & Hammerstein classic, big and lavish in the gilded Wang Theatre.
  • Late Nite Catechism: Til Death Do Us Part @ Charles Playhouse Theater 2 · Fri–Sun · $55 · Theater District
    Long-running audience-participation comedy: Sister's catechism class, this time on marriage.
  • Peter Pan Goes Wrong @ Lyric Stage Company · Fri–Sun · Back Bay
    A slapstick farce about a doomed amateur Peter Pan. The set falls apart on cue and the laughs don't stop.
  • Bill T. Jones/Arnie Zane Company: Collage Revisited and Story / @ ICA Boston · Fri–Sat · Seaport
    The legendary Bill T. Jones/Arnie Zane Company revisits landmark works at the ICA.
  • Dirty Dancing @ Emerson Colonial Theatre · Fri–Sun · Theater District
    The movie musical on stage, with all the songs and yes, the lift.
  • Chameleon Arts Ensemble – The Tipping Point: Ives, Ravel, Puts, Bartók @ First Church in Boston · Sat–Sun · $56 · Boston
    Ives, Ravel, Puts and Bartók from one of Boston's most inventive chamber groups.
  • Ballet BC @ Cutler Majestic Theatre · Sat–Sun · Theater District
    Vancouver's bold contemporary dance company, presented by Celebrity Series.
