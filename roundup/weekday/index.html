Hi Boston! Here are 5–9 curated things to do each day for Monday Nov 9 through Friday Nov 13, broken down by city (Boston, Cambridge, and Somerville).

Whether you're looking for live music, comedy, 35mm films, pub trivia, or free museum nights after work, here are the top picks for the week! Add anything else happening below.

🎭 RUNNING ALL WEEK

  • Wicked @ Citizens Opera House · Tue–Fri · Downtown Crossing
    The Broadway blockbuster's national tour at the Citizens Bank Opera House.
  • Hadestown @ Emerson Colonial Theatre · Tue–Fri · Theater District
    Anaïs Mitchell's haunting, Tony-winning underworld folk opera, at the Emerson Colonial through Nov 14.
  • You're A Good Man, Charlie Brown @ BCA Plaza Theatre · Wed–Fri · South End
    The Peanuts musical, up close at the BCA Plaza Theatre in the South End.
  • ‘Til Death Do Us Part: Late Nite Catechism @ The Charles Playhouse · Thu–Fri · $55 · Theater District
    Long-running audience-participation comedy: Sister's catechism class, this time on marriage.
  • THE BEAUTY QUEEN OF LEENANE @ Calderwood Pavilion · Thu–Fri · South End
    Martin McDonagh's pitch-black comedy-thriller about a mother and daughter in rural Connemara.
  • Peter Pan Goes Wrong @ Lyric Stage Company · Fri · Back Bay
    Slapstick farce from the creators of 'The Play That Goes Wrong', at the Lyric Stage.
