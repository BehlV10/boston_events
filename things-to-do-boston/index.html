Boston classics

The first-visit list, and still worth it the tenth time.

  • Walk the Freedom Trail
    A 2.5-mile red line through downtown and the North End past 16 Revolutionary-era sites. Walking it is free.
  • Boston Common & the Public Garden
    America's oldest public park and the garden next door, with the Swan Boats in the warmer months.
  • The North End
    Boston's Italian neighborhood: bakeries, red-sauce dinners, the Paul Revere House and Old North Church.
  • Fenway Park
    Red Sox games run spring through early fall; ballpark tours run year-round.
  • Faneuil Hall & Quincy Market
    Food hall, street performers and shopping by the waterfront.
  • Harvard Square & MIT
    Across the river in Cambridge: bookstores, Harvard Yard, the MIT campus and a busy music and comedy scene.

Museums

Most run free or discounted nights; our free events page lists them as they come up.

  • Museum of Fine Arts
    One of the country's great encyclopedic art museums.
  • Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum
    A Venetian-style palazzo built around a flowering courtyard, just down the Fenway from the MFA.
  • Institute of Contemporary Art
    Contemporary art on the Seaport waterfront.
  • Museum of Science
    Hands-on science, a planetarium and an IMAX dome at the Charles River dam.
  • New England Aquarium
    The giant ocean tank, penguins and harbor seals on Central Wharf.
  • Boston Public Library
    The Copley Square central library is free to visit: Bates Hall, murals and a courtyard.

Outdoors

Green space without leaving the city.

  • Arnold Arboretum
    Harvard's 281-acre tree collection in Jamaica Plain, free and open daily.
  • Charles River Esplanade
    Riverside paths for walking, running and sunsets along Back Bay.
  • Boston Harbor Islands
    Seasonal ferries to beaches, forts and campsites in the harbor.
  • Castle Island
    A loop walk around Fort Independence in South Boston, with planes landing overhead.
  • Minuteman Bikeway
    A rail trail from Alewife in Cambridge out through Arlington and Lexington.
