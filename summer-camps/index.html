How Mass Audubon's sliding scale works

Every Mass Audubon camp has five price tiers set by gross household income and family size. Tier 1 pays 20% of the full price; Tier 5 pays the full price. Mass Audubon doesn't publish the Tier 2–4 prices on its camp pages. All campers need a Mass Audubon membership: Tiers 1–3 get one free at registration, and Tiers 4–5 need a family-level membership.
